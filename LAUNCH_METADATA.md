Long description draft:

Barri is a browser tabletop horror game with an AI Case Curator. Open a case file, type what your investigator does, and let the Curator narrate scenes, track clues, call for d100 rolls, and remember the consequences of your choices.

The public demo is designed for a fast first session: no install, no account, and no prep. The full product direction is a cabinet of noir horror investigations that can be replayed through different approaches, suspects, and player decisions.
