# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Barri serves tabletop role-playing players and small groups who want an investigative horror session in the browser without preparing a human game master. Visitors arriving at the landing page are curious, skeptical, and often deciding quickly whether the product is a real playable tool or just atmospheric copy.

## Product Purpose

Barri lets players run browser-based tabletop horror investigations with an AI Case Curator that narrates scenes, tracks clues and consequences, handles d100-style rolls, and preserves campaign state. The landing page must make that promise concrete fast: playable demo, voice/text input, case files, persistent state, and horror tone.

## Brand Personality

Noir, procedural, uncanny. The brand should feel like a sealed case file from a strange bureau: tactile, investigative, and slightly dangerous, but still clear enough that visitors understand the product in the first viewport.

## Anti-references

Do not drift into generic AI SaaS: purple gradients, glass cards, bubbly icons, oversized metric panels, and repeated eyebrow scaffolding. Do not become illegible horror cosplay: muddy contrast, all-caps paragraphs, or decorative mystery at the expense of product clarity. Avoid generic Cthulhu/IP-forward presentation; Barri should read as its own Case Curator bureau.

## Design Principles

1. Show the playable product, not only the mood.
2. Let the case-file metaphor structure the page, but keep the evidence readable.
3. Use noir tension through typography, pacing, texture, and restrained color, not through low contrast.
4. Treat accessibility as part of the investigation: every clue, button, and file should be readable and reachable.
5. Keep AI involvement explicit without making the page look like a generic AI landing page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for text contrast and heading hierarchy. Motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. The landing page supports English, Ukrainian, and Spanish, so layouts must handle longer localized strings without clipping or horizontal scroll.
